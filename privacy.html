Kindlehaven Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Kindlehaven is a game by Wildheart Gaming for iPhone, iPad, Android and web browsers. This policy explains what information the game uses. In short: the game has no accounts and no ads, and it does not sell or share your personal information.

Information stored on your device

Your town, your hero, your settings and your progress are saved only on your own device, in the game's local storage. They are never uploaded by the app. Deleting the app (or clearing the browser's site data) deletes them.

Playing co-op

If you choose to play co-op, the game connects you directly to the other players in your room using a peer-to-peer connection. To set this up it uses the public PeerJS signalling service (peerjs.com), which, like any internet service, sees your device's IP address while it introduces players to each other. The players in your room see the hero name you chose, your hero's look (hair, beard, coat, hair and skin colours and gear) and your in-game actions. This information goes straight to those players and is not stored by us. Nothing is shared if you only play solo.

Information we collect

The iPhone, iPad and Android apps do not collect analytics, location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or any other personal data, and they contain no third-party advertising or tracking code.

When the game is played on claude.ai, a summary of each play session (hero name, time played, game progress and statistics) may be recorded to help balance the game. This does not apply to the mobile apps.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/dcardine/engage.

## Description

Lead a tiny settlement through an endless winter.

Chop wood, hunt and forage to keep your villagers warm and fed. Wall in your camp before nightfall, when raiders
come. Hire loggers, hunters, gatherers and haulers, and grow your camp from a few tents into a mighty stone castle.

CRAFT AND BREW
Collect hides, sinew, gems and magic materials from the creatures of the wilds. Forge weapons and armour, smelt iron,
steel and runesteel, and experiment in the pot to discover secret potions, meals and alloys.

EXPLORE A HUGE WORLD
Venture up to 1200 m from your town, past deadly beast lairs and monster camps, all the way to the mountains, where
epic dungeons hide the rarest loot.

TAME A COMPANION
Weaken a wolf, raptor, sabre tiger, T-rex or even an ice dragon and tame it. Pets level up and fight at your side.

PLAY WITH FRIENDS
Share a room code and build the town together in co-op.

- Survive nightly raids and three dragon sieges
- Seasons change the world around you
- 2D side-scrolling dungeons
- Quests, relics and legendary gear
- Plays offline (co-op needs a connection)
- No ads, no accounts
